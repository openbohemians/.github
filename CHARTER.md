# Open Bohemians Founding Charter

Draft for the founding launch.

Open Bohemians stewards useful, dependable open source software, helping
projects develop lasting maintenance and a community of contributors.
Our aim is to make useful projects easier to trust, contribute to, and
carry forward as their maintainers and users change.

## Projects we steward

A project belongs here when it has a clear purpose, a clear open source
license, and a maintainer willing to take responsibility for it. The
maintenance work must fit the time and people available. We begin with a
small collection and expand when we can support it well.

Projects keep their own technical direction, release practices, and
licenses. Libraries and their bindings can form one project family while
retaining separate repositories and release schedules.

## What stewardship means

Each stewarded project makes four things visible:

- **Responsibility:** named maintainers and an honest maintenance status.
- **Maintenance:** instructions for building and testing, a documented
  release process, and clear compatibility expectations.
- **Participation:** a place to report problems and instructions for
  contributing changes.
- **Continuity:** enough documentation for another maintainer to take over,
  and an explicit handover or retirement notice when maintenance ends.

Releases should describe changes that matter to users. Stable software may
need few commits; maintenance status reflects the maintainer's commitment,
not the volume of activity. Support is provided as time permits, with no
guaranteed response time.

## Decisions and maintainers

Tom ([@trans](https://github.com/trans)) is the founding steward and initially
makes organization-level decisions, including admitting projects. Project
maintainers make day-to-day technical decisions. Significant changes in
direction, compatibility, or maintenance are discussed and recorded in
public issues or pull requests.

Contributors can become maintainers through sustained, constructive work
and a willingness to share responsibility. A current maintainer proposes
the role and its scope; the founding steward records the appointment with
the contributor's agreement. As more people take responsibility, we will
revise this charter to share organization-level decisions.

## Joining and leaving

A project proposal identifies its purpose, license, maintainers, users, and
maintenance needs. Before admission, its maintainer supplies the basic
documentation above and records the project's status in the
[project register](PROJECTS.md).

The register distinguishes projects preparing for stewardship, maintained
projects, projects seeking a maintainer, and retired projects. Existing
repositories are reviewed individually against the same expectations.

When a maintainer steps back, the project records whether it is seeking
help, moving to another home, or retiring. Its history and last usable
release should remain available, with a clear notice for users.

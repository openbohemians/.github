# Sponsor Open Bohemians

Open Bohemians is building a lasting home for useful, dependable open source
software. We’re a small initiative founded by [Tom Sawyer](https://github.com/trans),
with the aim of growing shared stewardship as contributors become maintainers.

Sponsorship supports the time and care behind our projects: ongoing
development, bug fixes, testing, documentation, compatibility work, and
releases. Our goal is software people can rely on, with enough documentation
and community involvement to keep it useful over time.

We’re starting small and growing the collection as our capacity to maintain
it grows. You can explore the projects and our approach at
[openbohemians.org](https://openbohemians.org/).

Thank you for helping keep useful open source software going.
